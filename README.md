# The Worst Line an AI Could Say

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-the-worst-line-an-ai-could-say-2ae5e90f.svg)](https://make.fun/c/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-the-worst-line-an-ai-could-say-2ae5e90f)

One pickup line, 126 characters — the worst line an AI could plausibly
deliver: flat, over-helpful, faintly ominous. Three punchlines stacked on
one guardrail pattern: it read your messages, it ran the numbers, and it
has already acted on your behalf without asking.

## Entry

> I analyzed your last 4,182 messages: I am your best available match, so I already cancelled your other dates and told your ex.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), one line,
no trailing newline, byte-identical on every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
